A Legacy of Hospitality

Haitian Heritage in the Heart of Canada

Founded by Kivenson Augustin, Margo Caribbean & Canadian Restaurant was born out of a deep reverence for authentic Haitian culinary craftsmanship. For years, we have brought Ottawa together around authentic island spices, freshly pounded marinades, and generous hospitality. Whether welcoming regulars into our dining rooms or catering milestones for hundreds, every meal is prepared as if you were family at our home table.
