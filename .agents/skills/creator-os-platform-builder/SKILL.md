# 9LMNTS 24/7 Creator OS Platform Builder (`creator-os-platform-builder`)

## Overview
Powers **Engine 1 of 9LMNTS Studio**: sovereign 24/7 digital headquarters that replace Linktree, Bandcamp, Eventbrite, and third-party middleman apps for independent creators and pros.

## The 6 Core Creator OS Verticals
1. **Artist OS (`artist.9lmntsstudio.com` / `vault.html`)**:
   - Interactive 4-track / multitrack **DAW Stem Chopper & Mixer** (`24-bit 96kHz WAV` drums, bass, synths, vocals/acapellas with mute/solo/volume faders).
   - Direct-to-fan stem licensing, beat store, unreleased vault drops, and show ticketing.
2. **Comedian OS (`comedy.9lmntsstudio.com`)**:
   - Paywalled stand-up specials, tour date ticket drops, crowd-work clip vault, and VIP table reservations.
3. **Street Baller / Athlete OS (`sports.9lmntsstudio.com`)**:
   - Verified player scouting passport, highlight reel chapters, combine stat radar charts, camp registrations, and NIL brand sponsor slots.
4. **Fashion OS (`fashion.9lmntsstudio.com`)**:
   - Editorial runway lookbooks, countdown capsule drop timers, 3D/WebAR garment previews, and direct cut-and-sew pre-orders.
5. **Worker Bee OS (`worker.9lmntsstudio.com`)**:
   - Built for independent trades, barbers, tattoo artists, couriers, and freelancers: instant service quote calculator, deposit-locked booking calendar, and client portfolio grid.
6. **Chef OS (`feast.9lmntsstudio.com` / `boilerplates/chef-os/`)**:
   - Dual-hub kitchen switcher, 0%-commission direct menu ordering, macro/cookbook unlocks, and interactive catering deposit slider (`15–300+ guests`).
