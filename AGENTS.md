These rules govern the live `https://9lmntsstudio.com` build inside `live-9lmntsstudio-com/` and implement the locked Master Business Plan + Design Architecture.

## 1. Visual Polarity: Dark AI Showroom -> Native Client Firewall (Zero Bento)
- **Agency Shell (`#top`, `#pillars`, `#workflows`, `#pricing`, footer, modals)**:
  - Canvas: Deep Void `#050505` / Obsidian `#08090C`.
  - Accent: Flame Orange `#FF5500` (primary CTAs, `9L` mark, handwritten `+ AI` / `Studio` script, orbital rings) with subtle `#00D2FF` / `#00FF88` live status telemetry.
  - **Zero Bento Boxes**: Never wrap agency hero text or pillar descriptions in rounded rectangular card grids. Use floating typography, SVG hairline leader callouts, subtle ambient `9 Elements + AI` glyphs, and interactive `85vh` modals.
- **Client Brand Firewall (`#work` 5 Live Stages)**:
  - Each client stage renders in that industry's **native high-conversion aesthetic** (`#FAF6EF` warm Mediterranean sand for Shawarma, `#FFFFFF` gallery white for Specialty Coffee & Streetwear, `#F7F5F0` editorial cream for Bakery/Burgers, and `#08090C` cyan HUD for Project LOA / Sound Clash).
