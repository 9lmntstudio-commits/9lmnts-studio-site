## 6. Voice Synthesis & Text-to-Speech (TTS) Configuration

To achieve a consistent, broadcast-ready voiceover without manual recording, the channel deploys an enterprise-grade synthetic voice model (ElevenLabs, Cartesia, or CapCut TTS).

### Voice Persona & Tone Profile
* **Voice Archetype:** Tech Founder / Creative Director. Deep baritone, resonant, calm, confident, urban cadence. Professional without being stiff; relaxed without being casual.
* **Pacing:** 145–155 words per minute (crisp, articulate, leaving natural micro-pauses between assertions).
* **TTS Settings (ElevenLabs Standard):**
  * **Stability:** `0.65` (maintains authoritative tone without random robotic pitch drops).
  * **Clarity / Similarity:** `0.85` (ensures razor-sharp diction).
  * **Style Exaggeration:** `0.15` (natural vocal inflections, avoiding theatrical overacting).

> [!CRITICAL]
