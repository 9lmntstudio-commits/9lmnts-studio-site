Accessibility Statement

Last updated: October 20, 2026

9LMNTS Studio is committed to ensuring our digital platforms are accessible to all people, regardless of ability or technology. We engineer our interfaces following WCAG 2.1 Level AA standards.

  • High-contrast color tokens (exceeding 4.5:1 ratio for all body text)
  • Logical keyboard tab navigation and visible focus rings
  • Skip-to-content links on every page
  • Proper semantic HTML5 hierarchy and ARIA landmarks
  • Reduced-motion overrides for users with vestibular sensitivities

If you encounter any accessibility barrier on our site, please contact us at 9lmntstudio@gmail.com and we will address it promptly.
