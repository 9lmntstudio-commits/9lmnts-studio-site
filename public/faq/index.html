Clarity First

Frequently Asked Questions.

Straightforward answers about how we build, how much it costs, and how payments work.

Why do you use Interac e-Transfer instead of credit card checkout for deposits?

Credit card processors take 3% to 4% off every transaction and often hold funds for days. For Canadian B2B projects, Interac e-Transfer with autodeposit allows 0% merchant fees, instant 2-minute settlement, and direct fraud-proof bank-to-bank security. If you are an international client or specifically require corporate credit card invoicing, we can issue a Stripe checkout invoice upon request.

How fast can you really build and launch my website?

Our Quick Fix modular widgets are delivered in 2 to 5 business days. Our Launch Pages are ready in 1 to 2 weeks. Full multi-page Growth Websites take 3 to 4 weeks. We move fast because we design in modular sprints and work directly with decision-makers without agency bureaucracy.

Do I actually own my website after launch?

100% yes. Upon final milestone payment, you receive full ownership of all custom code, design assets, and content. You are never locked into proprietary platforms or forced into expensive ongoing contracts.

Do I have to pay a monthly retainer after the site is built?

No. Ongoing Care Plans ($199 / $449 CAD per month) are completely optional. If you prefer to manage hosting and updates yourself, we hand off all credentials and training videos at no additional charge.

What if I don't have professional photos or written copy?

All Growth and Signature packages include professional conversion copywriting tailored specifically to your Ottawa market. If you don't have photos yet, we source curated high-resolution photography and can recommend trusted local Ottawa photographers.

What is your refund policy?

Sprint deposits (40%–50%) are strictly non-refundable once development or strategic planning begins, as they compensate the studio for reserved calendar time. The remaining balance is only invoiced once you review and approve the final staging build prior to live domain launch.
