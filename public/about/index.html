Founder's Note

Most agency websites look pretty. Very few actually make the phone ring.

I started 9LMNTS Studio in Ottawa because I was tired of watching hardworking local restaurant owners, contractors, and event producers get burned by traditional agencies who charge $15,000 for slow, generic templates.

In 2026, a business website shouldn't just be an expensive online business card. It needs to be an automated booking engine that captures customer attention in 3 seconds, answers their burning questions, and secures their business before they drift away to another tab.

By blending modern AI engineering with clean minimalist design (the aesthetic standards of Linear and Vercel) and the raw energy of urban culture, we deliver enterprise-grade digital systems at prices independent businesses can actually justify.
