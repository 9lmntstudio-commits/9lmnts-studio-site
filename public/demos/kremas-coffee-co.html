Ancestral Roots · 1952

Born from
family kitchens.
Elevated for cold brew.

Kremas is not merely a drink in Haitian culture; it is the sacred heartbeat of celebration. Every December, family kitchens across Port-au-Prince and Jacmel fill with the sweet, intoxicating aromas of simmering sweet condensed cream, fresh-pressed coconut milk, and freshly ground whole spices toasted in seasoned cast iron.

We took that ancestral recipe and harmonized it with third-wave specialty coffee. By replacing heat-extracted espresso with a 18-hour cold brew steep, the natural sweetness and deep cacao notes of high-altitude cherries shine with silky clarity—without burning the delicate oils of the spices.
