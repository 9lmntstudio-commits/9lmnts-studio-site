BelFanm elevates the sacred Haitian ritual of Lwil Maskriti—gently roasting wild castor beans in cast iron before slow cold-pressing to release a thick amber nectar renowned for rebuilding damaged lipid skin barriers.

We partner directly with women-led farming syndicates in Les Cayes and Marmelade at 50% above Fairtrade baselines, micro-fluidizing these raw botanicals in Montreal into silky, non-greasy dermo-cosmetic elixirs.
