Architectural Manifesto

The Monolith Above The Clouds.

SOLARIUM represents a radical rejection of generic glass towers. Designed as a suspended monolithic pavilion on Mount Royal Ridge, every square foot is anchored by solid Roman travertine, hand-selected obsidian quartzite, and acoustic glass that eliminates metropolitan noise down to ambient forest stillness.

With only 4 bespoke private residences across the top 12 levels, each residence commands an uninterrupted 360-degree horizon with private dual high-speed elevators, temperature-controlled subterranean 4-car vaults, and direct access to the subterranean volcanic basalt thermal baths.
