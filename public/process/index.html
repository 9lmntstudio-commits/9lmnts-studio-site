How We Ship High-Impact Websites Without the Agency Headache.

Traditional agencies take 4 months and charge for endless meetings. We run focused, high-velocity sprints where you review working code from week one.

STEP 01

Onboarding & Friction Blueprint

Upon receipt of your agreement and initial 40%–50% sprint deposit, we immediately create your private staging portal and asset locker. We conduct a thorough audit of your competitors and extract your core service pricing.

  • Asset locker checklist (logos, photos, menus)
  • Mobile user flow mapping
  • Copywriting draft delivered within 72 hours
Days 1–4
STEP 02

Interactive Code Staging

We build your full website on a private, interactive staging URL. You can test your booking modals, submit test inquiries, and experience the exact mobile layout on your personal phone.

  • Responsive layouts tested on iPhone & Android
  • Supabase database and automated email routing setup
  • Consolidated client revision round
Days 5–18
STEP 03

Final Polish & Domain Go-Live

Once you approve the build, we connect your custom domain, install SSL certificates, verify DNS records, test live payment/booking rails, and hand over complete ownership.

  • Zero-downtime domain DNS propagation
  • Google Business & Search Console indexing
  • Recorded 10-minute video walkthrough of your system
Final Launch Day
